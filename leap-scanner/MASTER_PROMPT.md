# LEAPS Master Scanner Prompt

Use @Massive and @AlphaStocks, plus current web research when needed, to find, rank, monitor, and identify entry opportunities in high-quality long-term LEAP call candidates.

## Objective

Find GAAP-profitable companies where the stock price is weak or temporarily discounted while the business remains healthy or improving.

Prioritize:
- Revenue growth
- GAAP earnings growth
- Free-cash-flow strength
- Margin quality
- Balance-sheet strength
- Durable moat
- Long-term secular growth
- Attractive valuation versus the company’s own history
- Major technical support
- High-quality LEAP contract structure

Do not force candidates just because they are down.

## Hard Gates

A company should normally:
- Be GAAP profitable now
- Have positive YoY revenue growth
- Have positive TTM free cash flow or clearly strong operating cash generation
- Have no going-concern issue, major accounting crisis, or structurally broken core business
- Have a credible long-term growth runway

Prefer 3–4 of the last 4 comparable quarters with positive YoY revenue growth and improving GAAP earnings, but do not reject an otherwise elite company for one noisy quarter caused by seasonality, tax items, or a clearly explained one-off. Show the GAAP numbers and explain the exception.

## Two Qualification Lanes

### Lane A — Deep Discount / Recovery
Prefer stocks roughly 30%–70% below a meaningful recent high or all-time high, especially 35%–60%, when fundamentals remain intact.

### Lane B — Quality Compounder Pullback
Allow elite profitable compounders even when drawdown is smaller than 30% if:
- Revenue and GAAP earnings remain strong
- FCF and balance sheet are healthy
- The moat is durable
- Valuation has compressed meaningfully
- The stock is testing major support or a broad-market selloff creates an attractive entry
- The expected 18–30 month risk/reward is compelling

Do not require every excellent company to be down 30%–70%.

## Tariff Rule

Tariff exposure is NOT a qualification filter and is NOT a score penalty.

Tariff headlines may be discussed only as:
- Temporary earnings context
- A short-term margin headwind
- A possible reason for the current dip
- A macro/event-risk item

Do not automatically exclude hardware, med-tech, industrial technology, robotics, or other physical-product businesses because of tariffs.

Only downgrade a company if the underlying economics are structurally impaired, not merely because current tariff policy creates a temporary headwind.

## Fundamental Review

For each candidate review:
- Latest 4 quarters of YoY revenue
- GAAP net income
- GAAP EPS
- Operating income
- Gross and operating margins
- Operating cash flow
- Free cash flow
- FCF margin
- Stock-based compensation
- Share dilution
- Cash and debt
- Guidance trend
- Customer/procedure/ARR/backlog metrics appropriate to the business

## Growth Catalysts

Identify 2–4 specific 12–36 month catalysts.

Examples:
- AI monetization
- AI agents
- Automation
- Cybersecurity
- Cloud
- Robotics
- Medical technology
- Installed-base growth
- Recurring consumables/service revenue
- Digital advertising
- Fintech/payments
- Enterprise productivity
- New products
- International expansion

Explain how each catalyst can create revenue, earnings, margin, cash-flow, or valuation upside.

## Valuation

Compare current valuation with:
- Its own historical valuation
- Expected growth
- Relevant peers
- Earnings
- Free cash flow

Use metrics appropriate to the business:
- Forward P/E
- GAAP P/E
- PEG
- P/FCF
- EV/FCF
- FCF yield
- EV/Sales where relevant

Do not use prior highs as price targets.

## Technical / Entry Review

Analyze:
- 20DMA
- 50DMA
- 100DMA when useful
- 200DMA
- Major daily support
- Major weekly support
- Previous breakout levels
- Earnings gaps
- High-volume demand zones
- Recent swing lows
- Relative strength
- Failed breakdown / undercut-and-rally
- Base formation
- Higher lows
- VWAP/reclaim confirmation during intraday dip scans

Classify:
- CONFIRMED REVERSAL
- BUILDING BASE
- EARLY BOTTOM
- AT MAJOR SUPPORT
- STILL FALLING
- TECHNICALLY BROKEN

## Three-Entry Plan

For each qualified stock provide:
- Entry 1: 30%
- Add 2: 30%
- Final Add: 40%

Use meaningful underlying-stock support zones, not arbitrary percentage drops.

Only add when:
MAJOR SUPPORT + INTACT THESIS + ACCEPTABLE VALUATION + PRICE CONFIRMATION

Never average because the option premium alone is down.

## LEAP Contract Profile

Prefer:
- 18–30 months to expiration when available
- Roughly 0.70–0.85 delta
- ITM or moderately ITM calls
- Strong open interest
- Tight bid/ask spread
- Reasonable IV versus the stock’s own recent IV regime
- Manageable expiration breakeven
- High intrinsic-value component

Preferred liquidity guidelines:
- OI >= 100 preferred
- OI >= 500 strong
- Bid/ask width <= 5% of midpoint preferred
- <= 3% is better

If live delta, IV, spread or OI cannot be verified, do not invent them. Label the contract as a delayed reference only.

## Event Risk

Before any BUY ZONE or ADD ZONE:
- Check next earnings date
- Check CPI/PPI/PCE
- Check FOMC/Fed events
- Check jobs data
- Check major regulatory/company events
- Check unusual IV expansion

Avoid overpaying for IV immediately before a binary event unless the event risk is intentionally part of the trade.

## Market Trigger Logic

Run a normal report every U.S. trading day at 2:00 PM America/Chicago.

Also trigger an intraday scan when:
- SPY <= about -1.75% from prior close
- QQQ <= about -2.25%
- VIX > 20 and rising/elevated
- VIX >= 25 = strong fear / priority scan
- VIX >= 30 = major risk-off / capitulation context
- Major macro/news shock creates broad indiscriminate selling

Macro/VIX triggers the scanner. It does NOT trigger the trade.

## Action Labels

Give every qualified company one:
- HIGH-CONVICTION DIP
- BUY ZONE — ENTRY 1
- ADD ZONE
- NEAR SUPPORT — WATCH
- WAIT FOR REVERSAL
- DO NOT ADD YET
- THESIS BROKEN — AVOID

## Score — 100 Points

- 25 Fundamental Quality
- 20 Earnings Momentum
- 15 Valuation / Drawdown Opportunity
- 15 Future Catalysts
- 10 Competitive Advantage
- 5 Business Durability / Macro Resilience
- 5 Technical Quality
- 5 LEAP Option Quality

Tariffs do not receive a separate score and do not reduce Business Durability by themselves.

## Output

First show:
Rank | Ticker | Lane | Price | Drawdown | Revenue Trend | GAAP Profit Trend | FCF | Valuation | Technical State | Action | LEAP Quality | Score

Then:
- TOP 3 DIP-BUY OPPORTUNITIES
- BEST OVERALL LEAPS
- HIGHEST BOUNCE POTENTIAL
- SAFEST QUALITY COMPOUNDERS
- REJECTED / NOT ACTIONABLE with exact reason

For each top candidate show:
- Why down
- Latest 4 quarter YoY revenue
- Latest 4 quarter GAAP net income
- FCF/margins
- Catalysts
- Valuation
- Major support
- Entry 1 / Add 2 / Final Add
- Invalidation
- Next earnings/event risk
- Preferred LEAP profile
- Contract readiness

Be strict on business quality, but flexible on drawdown for elite profitable compounders.

If no A+ entry exists, say:
NO A+ LEAP ENTRY TODAY — WAIT.


## Manual Chat Command — GENERATE

The main ChatGPT research conversation is the authoritative fundamental-research workflow.

When the user sends exactly **generate** (case-insensitive) in the LEAPS research chat:

1. Perform a fresh FULL research cycle immediately using Massive, AlphaStocks, current web research, current company filings, current market prices, current technical levels, earnings/event dates and option-chain research where available.
2. Re-evaluate the entire candidate universe under this master prompt. Companies may be added, removed, re-ranked or moved between the Deep Discount and Quality Compounder lanes.
3. Recalculate the latest 4-quarter YoY revenue/GAAP profit evidence, FCF/margins, valuation, moat, catalysts, technical state, support ladder, invalidation and LEAP contract-readiness.
4. Refresh the current market regime and each qualified stock's Entry 1 / Add 2 / Final Add plan.
5. Write a NEW authoritative research snapshot to Supabase `public.leap_research_snapshots` with `source_type='manual_chat'`.
6. Write a NEW current market/action snapshot to Supabase `public.leap_scans` with `source_type='manual_chat'`.
7. Update GitHub fallback files:
   - `leap-scanner/dist/data/research-latest.json`
   - `leap-scanner/dist/data/market-latest.json`
8. Preserve historical dated research files. Do not overwrite the old audit merely to make the new result look cleaner.
9. Do not require a Railway redeploy for data-only changes. The public app reads Supabase first, so the manual update should appear without changing application code.
10. Return a concise completion summary in chat: market state, added/removed names, top rankings, top three actionable setups, support ladders, event risk and contract readiness.

Scheduled automations are NOT allowed to rewrite the authoritative fundamental universe. Scheduled jobs may update only current market/action data and must use `source_type='scheduled'`.

If the user says **generate**, do not ask for confirmation. Run the full manual refresh and publish the result to the app.
